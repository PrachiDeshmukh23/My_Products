🏢 Who We Are

PlantCare Fertilizers Pvt. Ltd. is a leading manufacturer and marketer of premium organic fertilizers based in Maharashtra. We are committed to providing scientifically formulated, high-quality organic solutions that boost crop productivity while maintaining soil health. Our products are manufactured by Soil Sanjeevan Organic Fertilizers Pvt. Ltd. at Rajgurunagar, Pune, and marketed across Maharashtra through our network of dealers and distributors.

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to make organic farming accessible and profitable for every Indian farmer. We believe that sustainable agriculture is the future — and we're working to make it the present. By combining traditional organic practices with modern science, we develop fertilizers that deliver real results season after season.

🌿 Our Organic Commitment

All PlantCare products are 100% organic and environmentally safe. We use PM Base Organic Manure, beneficial microbes, humates, amino acids, seaweed extract, and fulvic acid — never harmful chemicals. Our formulations improve soil structure, enhance nutrient absorption, and promote sustainable farming for future generations.

✅ Licensed & Certified

PlantCare Fertilizers operates under Fertilizer License No. LCFWD2026050740, fully compliant with Indian government regulations. All our products meet the strictest quality standards and are approved for agricultural use across India.
